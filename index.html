BENEFICIARY INCOME JOURNEY
Income through DIVA Design Studio compared with income after DIVA Design Studio.
X-axis: Studio  •  Y-axis: Beneficiary income
Navy = income through DIVA Design Studio  •  Teal = income after DIVA Design Studio.
Source note: Studio beneficiaries and Studio income use the reconciled Design Studio income sheets; production uses the Daily Update Tracker; After-DIVA income uses Studio responses only. Alumni are tracked separately and are not added again to unique beneficiaries.
Data audit (21 Oct 2026) — provisional, not fully reconciled: Headline income is based on workbook batch summaries.
